# Journey

## Early Tech Enthusiasm

Technology has always fascinated me. I started with electrical engineering – hardware, circuits, the physical side of technology. But relatively quickly it became clear: Computer Science is where I really belong. Software offers more flexibility, faster iteration, and the ability to solve problems on a completely different level.

---

## Studies at KIT & Founder Community

Computer Science studies at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). Focus: Cybersecurity and Machine Learning.

During my studies, I joined the founder student group **Innovate** in Karlsruhe. There I met many of my founder contacts who are still part of my network today. I also have some founders and self-employed people in my circle of friends – this community has definitely shaped how I think about entrepreneurship.

[Master Thesis](https://cdnc.itec.kit.edu/21_446.php) at [Chair of Dependable Nano Computing (CDNC)](https://cdnc.itec.kit.edu/24.php) on Power Side-Channel Attacks on Neural Network Accelerators. Research on data reconstruction through analysis of hardware power consumption.

Publication at IEEE FCCM 2023: ["Power2Picture: Using Generative CNNs for Input Recovery of Neural Network Accelerators through Power Side-Channels on FPGAs"](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10171482).

## Professional Experience - Consulting at PwC and Spike Digital Reply

After graduation, started at Spike Digital Reply as Security Consultant.

**Areas of responsibility:**
- Cloud Security Operations
- API Security & Penetration Testing
- DevSecOps Consulting
- Lead AI Security Taskforce
- Speaker at Vodafone Enterprise Plenum

**What I learned:**

Consulting was an excellent school. I gained experience in how enterprise companies and larger mid-sized companies function – their processes, their challenges, their decision-making paths.

At the same time, I got to know different technologies and problem areas. Each client had different systems, different requirements, different tech stacks. This diversity was extremely valuable.

## Current Projects

### Venedy

Together with [Armin Skollik](https://www.linkedin.com/in/armin-skollik-96a98a364/), I'm working on [Venedy](https://venedy.io) – an AI-powered API Security Testing Platform.

The idea originated from consulting experience: Manual API pentests take weeks and are costly. Conventional scanners miss Business Logic Vulnerabilities. With LLM technology, we automate the testing and make it accessible to companies of all sizes.

**Launch:** Q2 2026

### Security Consulting

In parallel, I offer Cybersecurity Consulting for selected clients – Cloud Security Audits, API security architecture, DevSecOps implementation.
